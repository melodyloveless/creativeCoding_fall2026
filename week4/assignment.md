## Iterative Plotting
A continuation of last week's assignment, focusing on recursive or iterative logic.

[Template](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1AY6WYAQd9fdKK4gMCzyxb4SqsqyxS-Fv/view?usp=drive_link){:target="_blank"}
- [GitHub](https://github.com/melodyloveless/creativeCodingFall2026_template/tree/main/week4){:target="_blank"}

Goals:
- To create a drawing that repeats a simple form into a generative pattern. Drawing inspiration from early computer artists like Molnar and Nake.
- To create a program that applies a particular transformation iteratively. 
- To generate an SVG file of this design that you will render using a pen plotter

Specs:
- p5.js sketch dimensions: up to you!
- Your plotted work must fit on a 8.5x11 piece of paper. You can resize your sketch/program using Inkscape.

Requirements:
- Your drawing must feature:
    - Some sort of iterative process (repetition + transformation/difference)
    - At least one of the following:
        - Randomness, activated by mouse input and/or key presses (ex. key press to generate a new version)
        - Interactivity, activated by mouse input (mouse position) and/or key presses. (ex. mouse position changes size)
    - Experiment with the aforementioned to manipulate an aspect of the drawing (color, size of certain elements, transformations, etc). Combine these techniques with map(), conditionals, and/or arithmetic.

Instructions: 
- You can re-use last week's drawing as a starting point or you can create a new drawing.
- After you are satisfied with your drawing, revise it for a pen plotter using the [p5.plotSvg library](https://github.com/golanlevin/p5.plotSvg)
- When finished, export your work from p5.js as an .svg file.
- Open your work in Inkscape. Plot using the iDraw plotter. Review our notes for more details.
- For your website:
    - Create a brief description of your drawing. 
    - Discuss: What are the main iterative processes of your drawing? Discuss your experiences with the pen plotter this week. What do you hope to accomplish/explore during your next drawing?
    - A link to your sketch
    - A photo of your plotted work
- Bring a physical copy of your drawing to next class. Be prepared to share your work with the class.

For this week's drawing, focus on getting familiar with the pen plotter and becoming satisfied with your work. (Don't feel like you have to aim for "perfect" results. Think about what you want to try out next and start planning for next time!)
