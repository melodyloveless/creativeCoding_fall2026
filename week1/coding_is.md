## Part Three: "Creative coding is..."

- Research different definitions for the term “creative coding”, keeping track of their sources. Make a list of these definitions + source pairs in a document.
- Using [this template](https://github.com/IDMNYU/What-is-Creative-Coding-template), create a github.io site that randomly generates definitions of creative coding replacing the quotes and sources with the items you found online. (Minimum: At least eight)
- You may modify the definition's wording in the template slightly, but the main subject should be present and what is shared should be relevant to the course. Keep the randomnization and interactivity.
- Bonus goal: Try to incorporate notes from class and our reading materials into this assignment.
- If you’re feeling spicy, try styling the page, adding or removing quotes to the template and making it your own.
- Using Github pages, upload your site to the web.
