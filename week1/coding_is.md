## Part Three: "Creative coding is..."

- Research different definitions for the term “creative coding”, keeping track of their sources. Make a list of these definitions + source pairs in a document.
- Using [this template](https://github.com/IDMNYU/What-is-Creative-Coding-template), create a github.io site that randomly generates definitions of creative coding replacing the quotes and sources with the items you found online. 
    - You may modify the wording in the orignal template slightly. Keep the randomnization and interactivity.
- Include a couple of sentences about your process and findings in the description of your project page.
- Bonus goal: Try to incorporate notes from class and our reading materials into this assignment.
- If you’re feeling spicy, try styling the page with CSS, adding or removing quotes to the template and making it your own.
- Using Github pages, upload your site to the web.
