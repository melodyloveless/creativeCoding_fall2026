## Interactivity Exploration

Optional: You may extend a previous sketch or assignment. If you do, link your original code and briefly list what you added or changed in your description. The new sketch must meet all requirements on its own.

### Sketch Requirements
- At least two different...
    - array methods (ex. push, slice, shift, unshift, etc.)
    - or string methods (ex. substring, split, etc.)
    - Bonus goal: try finding and including a method that was not discussed during class. I recommend browsing our notes and/or W3 Schools for more.
        - [W3 Schools: Array Methods](https://www.w3schools.com/jsref/jsref_obj_array.asp){:target="_blank"}
        - [W3 Schools: String Methods](https://www.w3schools.com/jsref/jsref_obj_string.asp){:target="_blank"}
- Interactivity (via mouse or keyboard)
    - This interaction should change the data structure itself (adding, removing, reordering, or filtering items).
    - This interaction should also produce a visible change in the sketch.
- A custom class with a constructor and at least one method.
- At least one instance of your class.
    - In other words, create an object using your class or "template"...
    - and incorporate your method(s) too.
    - Bonus goal: Instead of one instance, create multiple instances of your class, managed in an array.
